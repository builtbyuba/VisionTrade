## What it is

VisionTrade reads the market for you. It turns live price data for **gold (XAU/USD)** and **Bitcoin (BTC/USDT)** into a plain answer: what the structure is, who is in control, how volatile it is and what to watch next.

## Analysis

The current market state for each market, on the 15m, 30m, 45m and 1h timeframes:

- **Structure:** higher highs and higher lows, lower highs and lower lows, or a range, from confirmed price pivots.
- **Order flow:** whether buyers or sellers are driving the move.
- **Volatility:** how active the market is compared with its recent history.
- **Value:** where price sits against the value area of recent trading.
- **Evidence and risks:** what confirms the read and what could invalidate it.
- **Scenarios to watch:** the levels and conditions that matter next.

Analysis runs on settled candles, with the live quote alongside it.

## Day Signal

Once a session, the Day Signal engine scans the crypto futures market for the strongest setup:

1. **Scan.** Every liquid USDT perpetual, on several timeframes.
2. **Grade.** Each setup is scored on trend, structure, volume and risk, and graded A, B or C.
3. **AI review.** The top candidates are checked by an AI reviewer for target placement, stop placement and market regime.
4. **Publish.** The best setup is published with its entry, stop and three targets.

Every published signal is tracked to its result in a public **track record**: TP1 before the stop counts as a win, the stop first as a loss.
